    General Questions

    What is JusticeLine?
    JusticeLine is a secure, digital Complaint Management System that allows citizens to report grievances, crimes, civic issues, corruption, and other public safety concerns directly to authorities. The platform provides transparency through real-time status tracking and sends instant notifications to administrators.
    Is JusticeLine free to use?
    Yes, JusticeLine is completely free for all citizens. There are no fees or hidden charges for filing complaints, creating an account, or tracking your complaint status.
    Who can use JusticeLine?
    Any citizen who creates a registered account can use JusticeLine. You need a valid email address and phone number to register. There are no restrictions based on age, location, or background.
    What types of complaints can I file?
    You can file complaints under the following categories: Crime & Violence, Corruption & Fraud, Civic Issues, Environmental Violations, Traffic & Road Safety, Child & Women Safety, Property Disputes, and Other Emergencies. Visit our "How It Works" page for a full category breakdown.

    Account & Registration

    How do I create an account?
    Click "Register" in the navigation. Fill in your full name, phone number, email address, password, and residential address. Once submitted, you can immediately log in and begin filing complaints.
    Is my personal data secure?
    Absolutely. Your password is hashed using bcrypt industry-standard encryption — we never store your password in plain text. Your personal data is only visible to you and platform administrators who are responsible for addressing complaints. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
    What if I forget my password?
    Currently, you can contact our support team via the Contact page with your registered email to get assistance with account recovery. A self-service password reset feature is planned for future updates.
    Can I have multiple accounts?
    Each email address can only be associated with one account. Creating multiple accounts to misuse the platform is a violation of our terms and may result in all associated accounts being suspended.

    Filing & Tracking Complaints

    How long does it take to resolve a complaint?
    Resolution time depends on the nature and priority of the complaint. Critical and High priority cases are escalated immediately. Standard civic issues may take 3–14 business days. You can always track the status in real-time via the "My Status" page.
    Can I attach photos to my complaint?
    Yes. You can attach one image (up to 5MB in size) as photographic evidence when filing a complaint. Supported formats include JPG, PNG, and WEBP. We strongly recommend including evidence as it significantly speeds up the review process.
    What do the complaint statuses mean?
    Pending – Your complaint has been filed and is awaiting review by an authority.

    In Progress – An authority has reviewed your complaint and action is currently being taken.

    Resolved – Your complaint has been addressed and closed.

    Rejected – Your complaint could not be processed (may be a duplicate or outside our jurisdiction).
    Can I edit a complaint after submitting it?
    Currently, complaints cannot be edited after submission to maintain an accurate and unmodified record. If you need to provide additional information, please file a new complaint referencing your original Complaint ID, or contact support.
    What is the difference between priority levels?
    Critical – Life-threatening emergencies: active crime, fire, medical emergency.
    High – Serious incidents requiring urgent attention within 24 hours.
    Medium – Important issues needing attention within 1 week (default).
    Low – Minor inconveniences or suggestions for improvement.

    Emergency SOS

    When should I use the SOS button?
    Use the red EMERGENCY SOS button only in genuine life-threatening emergencies where you or someone nearby is in immediate danger. This triggers an instant alert via email to authorities AND logs the alert to the admin phone (9380268436). For non-urgent matters, please use the standard complaint form.
    Does SOS require me to be logged in?
    No. The SOS button is available on all pages and does not require a login. Anyone can trigger it by clicking the red button on any page of the website. You will be asked to optionally provide your name, phone, and emergency details.

    Admin Panel

    What are the default admin credentials?
    The default admin username is admin and the default password is admin123. It is strongly recommended to change these credentials after your first login by contacting the development team.
    What can admins do on the platform?
    Administrators can: view all complaints filed by all users, update the status of any complaint (Pending / In Progress / Resolved / Rejected), view photographic evidence, see GPS location data, view user information (name, phone, email), and delete spam or invalid complaint records. A statistics dashboard with charts is also available.
