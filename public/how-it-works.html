    How It Works

    Filing a complaint is simple, secure, and takes less than 3 minutes. Here's everything you need to know.

    Step-by-Step Process

    1

    Create Your Account

    Register with your name, email address, phone number, and residential address. Your password is encrypted with bcrypt — we never store it in plain text. Your account permanently links all your complaints for tracking.

    2

    Login Securely

    Sign in using your registered email and password. Your session is managed locally and securely. Only you can see your own complaints and personal data on the platform.

    3

    Submit Your Complaint

    Fill in the complaint form with a clear title, detailed description, incident date, category (e.g., Crime, Corruption, Civic Issue), and priority level. You can attach photographic evidence (up to 5MB) and allow GPS auto-capture of the exact incident location.

    4

    Automatic Authority Notification

    Once submitted, the system instantly sends an email alert to the platform administrators and relevant authorities with full complaint details. High-priority cases trigger immediate SOS-level escalation notifications.

    5

    Track Your Complaint Status

    Visit "My Status" in the navigation anytime to see the live status of every complaint you've filed. Status updates from Pending → In Progress → Resolved are shown in real-time as authorities take action.

    6

    Resolution & Closure

    Once an authority marks your complaint as Resolved, the status is updated permanently in our database. All records are stored long-term for audit and reference purposes. You may also file additional complaints at any time.

    Pro Tips for Effective Complaints

    Be specific: Include date, time, location, and names of involved parties if known. Vague complaints take longer to process.

    Attach evidence: Photos or screenshots substantially increase the credibility and speed of resolution of your complaint.

    Use GPS location: Allow location access so authorities can pinpoint the exact area. This is especially critical for crime reports.

    Choose the right priority: Reserve "Critical" and "High" priority for immediate life-safety threats. Misusing priority levels can delay genuine emergencies.

    Track your ID: Note your Complaint ID (e.g., #0023) for future reference. You can use it when contacting support.

    Use SOS for emergencies: If you're in immediate danger, use the red EMERGENCY SOS button — it sends an instant alert via email AND phone to authorities.
